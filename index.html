🧠 Instructions

  1. Click Start Camera and allow camera access. The music starts too.
  2. Hold up one hand, palm towards the camera.
  3. Start: show three fingers (thumb, index and middle finger up, the other two folded) and hold for a second.
  4. Now pinch your thumb and index finger together to turn the music down; spread them apart to turn it up. Keep the other three fingers folded.
  5. Stop: show the three fingers again. The volume stays where you left it.

💡 Tips

  • Use a well-lit room for better tracking.
  • It works near or far: the finger gap is compared with the size of your hand.
  • The ring around your palm fills while you hold the sign. Green = starting, orange = stopping.
  • The volume never changes while the sign is showing, or when no hand is seen.
  • The camera picture stays in your browser. Nothing is recorded.
